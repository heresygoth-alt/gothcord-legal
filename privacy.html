Gothcord Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, Gothcord Bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames, display names, roles, message counts, activity dates, virtual currency balances, inventories, and game data.

2. Message Content

The bot processes message content when necessary for features such as prefix commands, counting, moderation logging, and activity-based rewards. Deleted message logs may include message content, author information, timestamps, and channel details.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate bot features, maintain game progress, display profiles, support moderation, and administer the community.

4. Data Storage

Some information is stored in databases used by the bot. Access to stored information is limited to those responsible for operating and maintaining the bot.

5. Data Sharing

The bot does not sell personal information. Certain information may be displayed to other Discord members through profiles, leaderboards, game results, or moderation features.

6. Data Retention

Stored information may be retained while needed to operate the bot and its features. Retention periods depend on the type of information and operational requirements.

7. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of stored personal information by contacting the Gothcord administration team through the official Gothcord Discord server. Requests will be reviewed and handled as applicable.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the bot or its data practices. Updates will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Gothcord administration team through the official Gothcord Discord server.
